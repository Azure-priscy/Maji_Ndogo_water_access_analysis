# Maji Ndogo Water Access Project

## Problem Statement

Communities in Maji Ndogo and its surrounding areas face challenges in accessing safe and reliable water sources. Poor access to water can affect people's health, daily activities, and overall quality of life. To address these challenges effectively, decision-makers need data-driven insights to understand the current situation, identify the most affected communities, and prioritize investments that can improve water access.

## Business Objective

To analyze water access data from Maji Ndogo and surrounding communities, identify areas with the greatest need, understand the factors affecting water accessibility and reliability, and provide recommendations that can support informed infrastructure and resource allocation decisions.

## Analytical Objectives

* Assess the current state of water access across Maji Ndogo and surrounding communities.
* Identify communities most affected by poor accessibility, availability, reliability, and water quality.
* Investigate factors associated with poor water access.
* Highlight communities that may require priority intervention based on the severity and scale of the challenges they face.
* Recommend potential interventions based on the issues identified during the analysis.

## Analytical Questions

1. What is the current state of water access in Maji Ndogo and surrounding communities?
2. Which communities and populations are most affected by poor water accessibility and quality?
3. What factors are associated with poor water accessibility and quality?
4. Which communities should be prioritized for intervention based on the severity and scale of their challenges?
5. What interventions could help address the water-access challenges identified?

## Stakeholders

This project is intended to support:

* Government agencies responsible for water and infrastructure development.
* Non-governmental organizations (NGOs) working in water, sanitation, and community development.
* Development partners and donor organizations that fund water-related projects.
* Local authorities and district assemblies are involved in planning and implementing community interventions.

### Key Question

How can available resources be allocated effectively to improve access to safe and reliable water in the communities that need it most?
